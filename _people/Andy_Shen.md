desp: Andy Shen received the B.Eng. degree in Computer Science from Tongji University in 2024, and is currently pursuing the M.Sc. degree in Data Science at the PAMI Group, University of Macau, under the supervision of Prof. Bob Zhang, with expected graduation in 2027. Research interests include vision-language models, radiology report generation, and self-distillation. Current work develops pathology-aware curriculum and replay strategies for rare and long-tailed clinical findings, together with structured self-distillation methods for multimodal alignment and report fidelity, aiming to improve the clinical accuracy and robustness of automated radiology report generation.
