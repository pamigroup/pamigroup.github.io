## Postdoctoral Fellow in Computer Science

Annual salary of MOP 336,000. Macau's local tax exemptions reduce the effective tax rate
to roughly 5% to 7%, against a statutory maximum of 12%.

**Requirements**

- PhD degree holders who have graduated within 2 years.
- A proven record of outstanding academic achievement: publications, patents,
  presentations, academic awards.
- A PhD from a top-ranked university or discipline, that is, a Top 200 university in the
  THE/QS ranking, or one listed under the Double First-Class Initiatives and Programmes in
  Mainland China.

---

## PhD in Computer Science

Monthly stipend of MOP 12.5k to MOP 20k.

**Requirements**

- Bachelor's or Master's degree holders who have graduated or are expected to graduate
  before the start of their PhD studies.
- A proven record of outstanding academic achievement: publications, patents,
  presentations, academic awards.
- A Bachelor's and/or Master's degree from a top-ranked university or discipline, that is,
  a university listed in the THE/QS ranking, one listed under the Double First-Class
  Initiatives and Programmes in Mainland China, or a Project 211 / Project 985 university.
- Proficiency in English with a minimum score of IELTS 6.0, TOEFL 550, or CET-6 430.

---

## MSc in Computer Science

**Requirements**

- Bachelor's degree holders who have graduated or are expected to graduate before the
  start of their MSc studies.
- A proven record of outstanding academic achievement.
- A Bachelor's degree from a top-ranked university or discipline, that is, a university
  listed in the THE/QS ranking, one listed under the Double First-Class Initiatives and
  Programmes in Mainland China, or a Project 211 / Project 985 university.
- Proficiency in English with a minimum score of IELTS 6.0, TOEFL 550, or CET-6 430.
